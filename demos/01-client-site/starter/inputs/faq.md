# Approved FAQ
## Do we need to prepare?
Bring one real team challenge and invite people who can act on the decision.
## Can we join remotely?
Yes. Both formats can run remotely or in person.
## What accessibility support is available?
Tell us what would help when you enquire. We can provide captions, breaks and accessible digital materials.
## Does the form book a workshop?
No. It prepares a downloadable enquiry on your device. It does not send an email or confirm a booking.
