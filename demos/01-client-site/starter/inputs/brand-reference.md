# Design reference
A calm editorial site: warm ivory #FAF7F1 background, forest #284A3D headings, terracotta #B75D40 accents, charcoal body copy.
Use a large serif title, generous whitespace, clear comparisons and a useful enquiry form. Avoid gradients, generic stock photos and a wall of cards.
Show the two packages with duration, capacity, outcome and price. Use accessible labels and a mobile layout. A simple self-contained index.html with inline CSS/JS is sufficient.
